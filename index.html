FAQ

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on project scope, complexity, and timeline. Simple landing pages start at one rate, while complex web applications require custom quotes. I offer fixed-price packages for standard projects and hourly rates for ongoing maintenance. Contact me with your project details for an accurate estimate.

What's the typical project timeline?

A simple landing page typically takes 1-2 weeks, while more complex websites or dashboards can take 3-6 weeks depending on features and content. I provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation and keep you updated throughout the development process.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! All projects include 2 rounds of revisions to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final result. I'm committed to delivering exactly what you envision, and I'm flexible throughout the process to accommodate feedback and adjustments.

What technologies do you use?

I specialize in a modern frontend stack designed for speed and maintainability. I primarily work with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and TypeScript. For complex applications, I use Vue.js to build reactive user interfaces

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

Absolutely! I work with businesses of all sizes—from startups launching their first website to established companies needing redesigns or new features. I tailor my approach to fit your budget, timeline, and growth goals.

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?

I can help you set up hosting on platforms like Netlify, Vercel, or GitHub Pages, and provide guidance on domain registration. I also offer ongoing maintenance packages for updates, bug fixes, and technical support after launch.
